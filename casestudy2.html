FIGMA UX/UI PROTOTYPING

Problem Statement

Volunteer coordination connects registrations, teams, shifts, qualifications, permissions, and event-day operations.

When these workflows are disconnected, volunteers can struggle to find and manage commitments while organizers spend more time coordinating people and event requirements.

User Research

I used legacy event information to understand how teams, shifts, qualifications, and event requirements were organized.

Conducted primary research, qualitative and quantitative questionnaires, user testing, and feedback from previous volunteers/admins. Checked for constraints & accesability.

These inputs informed an experience for simpler volunteer sign-up and more independent event management.

01 — EXPERIENCE

Volunteers need a simple way to discover teams, browse shifts, and manage sign-ups.

Design opportunity: Bring team discovery, shift browsing, and sign-ups into a clear volunteer experience.

02 — OPERATIONS

Organizers need tools to manage teams, shift capacity, qualifications, permissions, and volunteer information.

Design opportunity: Connect team, shift, and volunteer administration in one place.

03 — SCALABILITY

Organizations have different event structures, qualifications, and volunteer requirements.

Design opportunity: Make the platform configurable for different organizational needs.

User Journey & Usability

Volunteers and organizers need distinct workflows that work together:

  • Volunteers: Access the platform, find teams, browse shifts, sign up, and manage commitments.
  • Organizers: Configure events, manage teams and shifts, coordinate volunteers, and maintain event information.

Clear navigation, consistent interactions, useful feedback, and fewer unnecessary steps help keep tasks straightforward.

Design Direction

Helping you do, less.

Connecting volunteer participation with the tools organizers need to run events. Considerations: System updates, data collection,imports and exports.

Information Architecture

The platform is organized around two connected sets of tools.

  • Volunteer tools: Dashboard, teams, shifts, sign-ups, profile, and qualifications.
  • Administrative tools: Event setup, team and shift management, volunteer information, and permissions.

Configurable events, teams, shifts, qualifications, and permissions help the platform adapt to different organizations.

Bringing It Together
