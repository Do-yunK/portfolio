ESTEEM

A multilingual marketplace for local services

Project Description

ESTEEM evolved from my earlier KTown app concept into a multilingual service marketplace connecting clients with local businesses and contractors. The concept broadens service discovery through language-aware information and communication, helping people from different linguistic and cultural backgrounds explore local providers.

Problem Statement

Finding and hiring local services can be difficult when listings are hard to understand, platforms are unfamiliar, or communication options are limited. People need to understand their choices, explain their needs, and connect with providers across language differences.

How might we make discovering and hiring local services more accessible across different languages and communities?

User Research

The concept considers language accessibility, communication, service discovery, and trust as connected parts of finding local help. It focuses on making service information easier to understand and helping clients approach providers with greater confidence.

01 — Language Barriers

People may struggle to understand service listings or explain what they need to providers.

Design opportunity: Make language preferences and translated communication part of the service experience.

02 — Service Discovery

Finding relevant local businesses and contractors can be difficult without clear categories.

Design opportunity: Organize services and listings to make relevant providers easier to explore.

03 — Building Trust

Choosing and contacting an unfamiliar service provider can feel uncertain.

Design opportunity: Present clear business and service information to support informed decisions.

User Journey & Usability

Discover local services, explore businesses and contractors, understand service information across language preferences, communicate with providers, and compare available information before deciding. Clear navigation, understandable language options, and consistent interactions support each step.

Hero Statement

Making local services easier to discover, understand, and access.

ESTEEM connects people with local providers through a more inclusive, multilingual service-discovery experience.

Information Architecture

The app organizes service discovery, business information, language preferences, and provider communication into a clear path from exploring options to contacting a service provider.

Interface Design

Bringing It Together

ESTEEM builds on KTown by broadening the concept toward inclusive service discovery and multilingual communication. It connects clients with local providers, with understandable information and trust considered throughout the experience.

Conclusion

Evolving KTown into ESTEEM showed how an existing concept can be reshaped around more accessible service discovery. Thoughtful language and communication choices can reduce barriers and make everyday services easier to explore across different communities.
